## Control

Rule: For security requirements, Emberline Commercial Bank may not mark credit approval complete until KYC and AML Officer verifies beneficial ownership certification, sanctions results, and source-of-funds review.
Owner: KYC and AML Officer
Required evidence: beneficial ownership certification, sanctions results, and source-of-funds review.
Approval threshold: Exposure above USD 1,000,000.00 (100000000 minor units) requires written approval from KYC and AML Officer.
Escalation trigger: Escalate when a beneficial owner is unverified or a screening alert remains open.

## Recordkeeping

Attach the evidence to the credit approval record, preserve prior versions, and record the decision date before advancing the commercial lending and treasury sale.
